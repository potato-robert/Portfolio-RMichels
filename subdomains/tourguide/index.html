Discover tours, plan your trip, and explore with confidence

Tourguide helps you find community-built walking tours, navigate stop by stop, and chat with an AI assistant when you need local tips on the go.

Features

Everything you need for your next adventure

From planning to exploring, Tourguide brings maps, stories, and local knowledge together in one cross-platform app.

Discover Tours

Browse tours created by travelers worldwide. See duration, themes, and highlights before you go, so you know what to expect at every stop.

Create & Share

Build your own tours, add personal insights at each location, and share them with the community. Review and comment on tours from other explorers.

AI Tour Guide

Chat with an AI assistant for destination tips, itinerary ideas, and answers while you travel. Get recommendations tailored to your plans.

How it works

From inspiration to exploration in three steps

  1. 1

    Find a tour

    Search destinations and filter by themes like scenic, urban, or food. Read descriptions, see maps, and check community feedback.

  2. 2

    Follow the route

    Open a tour and move through each stop with map guidance, photos, and context. Jump ahead or revisit places at your own pace.

  3. 3

    Share what you learn

    Rate tours, leave comments, and publish your own routes so other travelers can benefit from your local discoveries.

Built for travelers

Cross-platform, community-driven, and always improving

Tourguide is a Flutter app with a polished Material 3 interface, dark mode support, and a growing library of user-created content. Use it on Android or in the browser — your tours and profile stay in sync.

  • Google Maps integration with custom markers
  • Firebase-backed accounts and community features
  • Responsive web experience at tourguide-firebase.web.app
  • Active beta with regular updates
