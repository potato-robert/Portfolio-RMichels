<section class="sectionText">
  <h2>Context and problem</h2>
  <p>Practitioners use Canfit to design custom prosthetic and orthotic devices. They shape each device from a 3D patient scan, from manual measurements, or from both. The shape work is buildups, reductions, alignment, and other clinical changes that would be difficult or impossible in plaster alone. A finished design exports as a shape file for 3D printing or CNC, including sockets, ankle braces, and custom insoles.</p>
</section>
<section class="sectionText">
  <h2>My role and approach</h2>
  <p>I am a Software Developer at Qwadra Vancouver, and I work on Canfit. The product is a shared C++ and MFC codebase. I contribute to clinical modifier workflows, 3D visualizations, macros, networking, and UI in that shared codebase. On the modifiers, I connect design and engineering with controls that stay faithful to clinical intent. The piece I owned outright is the PHP configuration API. I designed and implemented it end to end, and it delivers update alerts and other dynamic content to Canfit.</p>
</section>
<section class="sectionText">
  <h2>Key work</h2>
  <h3>Clinical shape changes</h3>
  <p><strong>Problem.</strong> Practitioners need buildups, reductions, alignment, and other clinical shape changes that would be difficult or impossible in plaster alone.</p>
  <p><strong>Decision.</strong> I contribute to the shape modifiers for those changes in the shared C++ and MFC codebase.</p>
  <p><strong>Result.</strong> Practitioners make those changes on the digital device.</p>
  <h3>Macros</h3>
  <p><strong>Problem.</strong> Clinicians need to record a sequence of modifications, replay it later, and edit it on playback.</p>
  <p><strong>Decision.</strong> I work on macros that record a sequence, replay it, and allow an edit on playback.</p>
  <p><strong>Result.</strong> Clinicians get automation with consistent behavior suited to clinical use.</p>
  <h3>Configuration API</h3>
  <p><strong>Problem.</strong> Customers need update alerts and other dynamic content so they can stay current and reach sales and support.</p>
  <p><strong>Decision.</strong> I designed and implemented a PHP configuration API, and I owned that backend end to end.</p>
  <p><strong>Result.</strong> The API delivers those alerts and that content to Canfit.</p>
</section>
<section class="sectionText">
  <h2>One modifier, end to end</h2>
  <p>One modifier, end to end, is the path from a clinical shape change, through the control, to the shape file that exports. I contribute to that path in the shared codebase. Practitioners can rotate, align, twist, and refine the device with those controls, and the controls stay faithful to clinical intent. That file is the export for 3D printing or CNC.</p>
